Cancellation & Refund Policy

Customer cancellation

Cash-on-delivery orders can be cancelled while they are in the eligible early stages shown in My Orders. Once an order has progressed beyond that stage, cancellation may not be available.

Online payments

If online payments are enabled, refunds for eligible cancelled or failed transactions will be handled according to the payment provider and Purez refund process.

Product issues

For damaged, incorrect or missing items, contact Purez support with the order number and details as soon as possible after delivery.
