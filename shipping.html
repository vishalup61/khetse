Shipping & Delivery

Purez currently focuses on Ghazipur, Uttar Pradesh and selected nearby delivery areas.

Delivery charge

Standard delivery is ₹30 for eligible orders below ₹300. Orders of ₹300 or more are eligible for free delivery, subject to serviceability.

Delivery area

Coverage can vary by locality and pincode. If an address is outside the active delivery area, Purez may contact the customer before accepting the order.

Delivery timing

Delivery timing depends on product availability, seller preparation and local conditions. The customer will receive order-status updates through the available Purez channels.
